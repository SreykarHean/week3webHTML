About Livre Café

A cozy place where coffee, books, and nature come together. Our café is made for people who love quiet mornings, warm drinks, and relaxing with a good book.

The idea of Livre Café began with a simple dream. to build a place where people could pause from the city's rush and enjoy the little things.

We wanted to create a space filled with the scent of coffee, the softness of old pages, and the calmness of nature.

Every corner of our café has its own charm, with vintage furniture, soft music, and free books to read while you enjoy your drink.

Our Values

  • Creativity and comfort in every detail
  • Freshly brewed, high-quality coffee
  • Handmade baked goods
  • A love for books, art, and learning
  • Eco-friendly and community-focused
